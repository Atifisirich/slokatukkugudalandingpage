Foundations & Skill Building

Preparatory Stage Grades 3 to 5 · Ages 8–11

Strong foundations in Mathematics, Science, English, and Environmental Studies (EVS) are built through activity-based learning, reading, and writing, supported by Wordsworth AI language tools.

What a Preparatory stage student will do

    Sloka Tukkuguda AdmissionsAnswers to the questions parents ask most

    What curriculum does Sloka International School, Tukkuguda follow?
    S

    We follow the CBSE curriculum integrated with modern experiential learning frameworks, aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP 2020).

    What stages and grades are offered at the Tukkuguda campus?
    S

    We cater to students across all primary and secondary learning stages:

    • Foundational Stage: Nursery to Grade 2 (Ages 3–8)
    • Preparatory Stage: Grades 3 to 5 (Ages 8–11)
    • Middle Stage: Grades 6 to 8 (Ages 11–14)
    • Secondary Stage: Grades 9 & 10 (Ages 14–16)
    What is the student-teacher ratio?
    S

    We maintain an optimal student-teacher ratio (typically between 15:1 and 25:1 in early years, and up to 30:1 in higher grades) to ensure every child receives personalized attention, continuous mentoring, and individual academic support.

    Are the classrooms air-conditioned and digitally equipped?
    S

    Yes, all classrooms are fully air-conditioned to maintain a comfortable, conducive environment for year-round learning. Additionally, classrooms are equipped with smart digital boards and interactive technology, allowing educators to blend traditional teaching with multimedia content, simulations, and digital learning tools.

    What kind of seating and furniture is provided in the classrooms to ensure student comfort?
    S

    We use age-appropriate, ergonomically designed tables and chairs tailored to the specific grade and age group of our students. By adjusting furniture dimensions to match children's growing physical proportions, we support healthy posture, prevent fatigue, and ensure students can sit and focus comfortably throughout the academic day.

    What practical lab facilities are available for students in higher grades?
    S

    Middle and Secondary stage students have access to dedicated science laboratories, including full-scale Physics, Chemistry, and Biology labs for practical coursework and inquiry-based learning.

    Does the school provide transport facilities, and how can parents inquire about specific routes?
    S

    Yes, we offer safe, air-conditioned bus transport equipped with GPS tracking for real-time safety. We cover approximately 38 pickup and drop-off points for our students. For specific route inquiries and detailed transport schedules, please contact our Transport Manager directly at +91 80196 46415.

    Search pick-up points and distance

    How are young children cared for on campus, and what is the background of the support staff?
    S

    We have trained, attentive nannies and support staff who can communicate in basic English. They are dedicated to caring for our younger students, ensuring their safety, hygiene, and personal comfort throughout the school day.

    Who is the point of contact for parents during school hours regarding academic or student matters?
    S

    During school hours, parents can connect directly with their respective Academic Incharges. We have dedicated Incharges for Pre-Primary, Primary, and Higher Grades to ensure swift, specialized support and effective communication for each age group.

    How frequently can parents meet the Principal, and what is the process?
    S

    Parents can meet the Principal whenever needed by scheduling a prior appointment. To ensure focused time for every discussion, meetings are coordinated through the school office by booking a slot in advance.

    How frequently are Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTMs) conducted?
    S

    Parent-Teacher Meetings are conducted regularly throughout the academic session to review student progress, discuss academic performance, and address any developmental needs. PTM dates are scheduled and communicated in advance through the school calendar to ensure active collaboration between parents and teachers.

    What sports and co-curricular facilities are offered at the campus?
    S

    We offer a rich variety of sports, arts, and creative learning opportunities across dedicated spaces:

    • Field Sports & Athletics: A dedicated football court, athletics track, and cricket nets.
    • Court Sports: Basketball court, a dedicated skating rink, and pickleball courts.
    • Indoor Sports: A multi-sport indoor space for chess, carrom, and table tennis.
    • Performing Arts & Visual Arts: Spacious and creative studios dedicated separately to Music, Dance, and Art & Craft
    How does the school ensure child safety and security on campus?
    S

    Child safety is our highest priority, backed by comprehensive monitoring and strict campus access protocols:

    • 24/7 CCTV & Intercom Systems: The campus is equipped with over 130 CCTV cameras covering all critical areas, along with intercom systems for instant campus-wide communication.
    • Digital Gate Pass & QR Approval System: To ensure authorized access, parents or guardians leaving campus with a student must go through a secure digital gate pass system requiring QR code approval before exiting.
    • Monitored & Safe Spaces: All indoor and outdoor learning areas, play facilities, and entry/exit points feature child-friendly infrastructure and continuous physical surveillance to ensure a safe environment at all times.
    How does the school communicate with parents regarding daily updates, announcements, and events?
    S

    We maintain clear and timely communication with parents through multiple channels:

    • Sloka Mobile App: Used as our primary digital platform for real-time announcements, daily updates, circulars, event notifications, academic tracking, and school activities.
    • Phone Calls & WhatsApp: Used for direct, urgent, or quick personal updates regarding your child.
    • Email: Used for formal communications
    How are parent concerns addressed and resolved by the school?
    S

    We have a structured, multi-tier support system to ensure every parent's feedback or concern is addressed with prompt attention and empathy:

    • Dedicated Listening Team: Parent queries and concerns are logged and systematically managed by our dedicated parent support team to ensure timely follow-up.
    • Academic Incharge Resolution: Concerns regarding academics, classroom progress, or daily student well-being are routed directly to the respective Pre-Primary, Primary, or Higher Grade Academic Incharges for immediate coordination with teachers.
    • One-on-One Meetings: For more detailed or sensitive matters, parents can schedule one-on-one interaction sessions with the Academic Incharge or the Principal via prior appointment.
    What Makes Sloka International School Unique, and Why Should Parents Choose Us?
    S

    At Sloka, we believe that every child is unique and has something special within them. Our responsibility is to recognise that potential and provide every child with the opportunity to grow, learn, and shine.

    We don't want children to come to school just to learn lessons and score marks. We want them to develop confidence, curiosity, discipline, strong values, and the courage to express themselves.

    We understand that every child learns differently. Some need encouragement, some need individual attention, and some simply need someone to believe in them. We strive to understand every child and support them at every step.

    Along with strong CBSE academics, we give equal importance to sports, creativity, communication, leadership, and essential life skills.

    Most importantly, we believe parents are our partners. Your child's happiness, safety, growth, and future matter to us just as much as they matter to you.

    When your child walks into Sloka, we want you to feel that you haven't just chosen a school; you have chosen a second home where your child's dreams matter.

    At Sloka, we don't just prepare children for examinations; we prepare them for life. Our dream is to see every child grow into a confident, independent, responsible, and compassionate individual.

    Because every child deserves a place where they belong, believe, and become!
