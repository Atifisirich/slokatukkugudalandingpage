Sloka Tukkuguda AdmissionsAnswers to the questions parents ask most

Which grades does Sloka Tukkuguda offer?
S

Sloka International School, Tukkuguda is a CBSE school offering classes from Nursery to Grade 10, with a Montessori-inspired Early Years programme.

Is the curriculum CBSE and NEP 2020 aligned?
S

Yes. The school follows the CBSE curriculum, aligned with NEP 2020, with activity-based and technology-integrated learning.

What is the admission process for 2027–28?
S

Book a campus visit online or by phone, meet the admissions counsellor and tour the campus, then complete the application and submit the required documents.

Book a campus visit

Is school transport available?
S

Yes. Transport covers nearby areas including Imamguda, Gangaram, Sreenagar, Shaheen Nagar, Pedda Golconda, Balapur, Maheshwaram, Mankhal, Raviryala, Kongara Kalan, Adibatla and Mucherla. Please confirm your route with the admissions team.

Check the distance from your area

How far is the school from Adibatla, Kongara Kalan and Maheshwaram?
S

Approximately 10–12 km from Adibatla, 8–10 km from Kongara Kalan and 6–8 km from Maheshwaram.

Is the campus safe?
S

The campus has CCTV surveillance across classrooms, corridors, entry and exit points and outdoor areas, with a supervised environment throughout the day.

What sports facilities are available?
S

A football ground, basketball courts, a skating rink and a cricket ground, with sports as part of every student's regular schedule.

What are Words Warrior, Podcast and Spinning Tales?
S

Sloka's communication programmes. Words Warrior is a gamified vocabulary programme, Podcast has students script and record their own shows, and Spinning Tales builds storytelling skills.

What is the Aero Bay Innovation Lab?
S

An aerospace-inspired innovation lab where students work on robotics, coding, AI projects and hands-on STEM challenges.

What is the Early Years programme like?
S

A Montessori-inspired, play-based programme with a dedicated Play Land and Activity Hall, where children learn through exploration and sensory play.
