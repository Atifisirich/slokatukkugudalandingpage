Privacy Policy

Sloka International School, Tukkuguda · Last updated 2 November 2026

In short

  • We collect what you type into our enquiry form, plus basic facts about how you used this page.
  • We use it to call you back about admissions and to see which pages and ads bring families to us.
  • We do not sell your information.
  • You can ask to see it, correct it, delete it, or stop us contacting you, at any time.

1. Who we are

This policy covers the admissions page of Sloka International School, Tukkuguda ("the school", "we", "us"). The school is at 6F5H+4M5, Tukkuguda, Maheshwaram, Telangana 501359 (Exit ORR 14, Laxmi Nagar, opposite Sri Visista Hospital Lane). [CONFIRM THE SCHOOL'S LEGAL NAME / MANAGEMENT ENTITY]

It applies to this page and the enquiry form on it. The school's main website, slokainternational.com, has its own notices.

2. What we collect

What you give us

When you send the "Book a Campus Visit / Request a Call Back" form we ask for:

  • the parent or guardian's name
  • the student's name
  • your mobile number, and your e-mail address if you choose to give it
  • the grade your child will join and the area you live in
  • the characters you type to show you are not a robot

If you call, WhatsApp or e-mail us, we also receive your number or address and whatever you tell us.

What the page collects automatically

  • Pages and sections viewed, the type of device and browser, and your approximate location (from your internet address).
  • How you reached us: the page you came from, and any ad details in the web address (such as utm labels and ad click codes). We keep these for your visit and send them with your enquiry so we know which ad worked.
  • A few actions: the grade you pick, the area you check, the FAQ topics you open or search for, and taps on the call and WhatsApp buttons.

Children

This page is for parents and guardians. We ask for a student's name and grade only so we can plan the admission, and we get them from the parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children, and we do not aim advertising at them.

3. Why we use it

  • To call, message or e-mail you about your enquiry and to arrange a campus visit.
  • To run the admission process if you go ahead, including applications and documents.
  • To answer questions you send us.
  • To understand which pages and ads bring families to the school, and to improve this page.
  • To keep records and meet legal duties.

We contact you only about admissions at the school unless you agree to something else.

4. Your consent

By ticking the consent box and sending the form, you agree that the school may contact you about your enquiry and use your details as described here. You can take that consent back at any time by telling us (see section 11). We will then stop contacting you, although we may keep a note that you asked us to.

5. Who we share it with

  • The school's admissions team, who respond to your enquiry.
  • The company that provides our enquiry form and CRM (ExtraaEdge), which hosts the form and stores enquiries for us.
  • Our website hosting provider, which delivers this page.
  • Measurement and advertising tools loaded through Google Tag Manager, which help us see which ads and pages work. [CONFIRM WHICH TOOLS ARE SWITCHED ON, e.g. GOOGLE ANALYTICS, GOOGLE ADS, META PIXEL]
  • Google (Maps and Fonts) and WhatsApp when you use them from this page. They receive details such as your internet address, and their own policies apply.
  • Authorities or courts, if the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information. Some of these providers may store or process information outside India.

6. Cookies and browser storage

  • The page uses short-term browser storage to remember that you have already seen the loading screen and to hold the ad details of your visit. It clears when you close the tab.
  • The enquiry form and the tools loaded through Google Tag Manager may set cookies to make the form work and to measure visits and ads.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The page will still load, but the form or our measurement may not work fully.

7. Links and embedded content

This page links to or embeds other services: Google Maps, WhatsApp, the school's main website and the CBSE Mandatory Disclosure page. We do not control them, and you should read their own privacy notices.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your enquiry details only for as long as we need them to follow up on your admission enquiry, and for school records we are required to keep. After that we delete them or remove anything that identifies you. [CONFIRM HOW LONG ENQUIRIES ARE KEPT, e.g. 12 MONTHS]

9. Keeping it safe

We use reasonable safeguards: this page is served over HTTPS, and only the admissions team and the providers named above can see enquiry details. No online service is completely secure, so please do not send us information we have not asked for, such as bank or card details.

10. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information of yours we hold and how we use it
  • correct, complete or update it
  • delete it, once we no longer need it
  • stop using it, by withdrawing your consent
  • deal with a complaint (see the contact details below)
  • name another person to act for you if you cannot, for example in case of death or incapacity

Write or call using the details below and tell us what you would like. We may ask you to confirm who you are first. We will reply as soon as we reasonably can. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operating.
